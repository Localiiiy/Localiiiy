Delete My Account Permanently

Use this official portal to permanently and irreversibly delete your Localiiiy account and all associated cloud and local data across our Android App and Web App.

Signed in
You can permanently erase your active account in one click below.

What Data Is Permanently Erased

When you submit permanent account deletion, the following records are purged from Google Cloud Firebase (localiiiy) and your device:

  • Firebase Authentication Credentials: Your email/password or Google OAuth identity record (auth.currentUser.delete()).
  • Firestore User Profile & Handle: Your users/{uid} profile document and usernames/{handle} reservation.
  • Live Spatial Radar Beacons: Any active radar_beacons/{uid} proximity pin is immediately deleted.
  • User-Authored Content: Your Pulse posts, short-form Clips, Studio videos, Marketplace listings, and Connections graph.
  • Zero Retention Period: Account deletion executes immediately for live authenticated sessions, or within 24 hours for email-verified erasure tickets, with zero commercial data retention.

In-App Deletion Steps (Android & Web)

You can also trigger instant 1-tap permanent deletion directly inside the Localiiiy Android APK or Web App:

  • Open Localiiiy and go to the Space (Profile) tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Account Permanently.
  • Confirm the prompt to immediately wipe both cloud Firestore records and local device storage.
