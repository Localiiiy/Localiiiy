🗑️ Delete Your Localiiiy Account & Wipe Data

We respect your privacy and provide immediate mechanisms to delete your user profile, media, clips, and spatial location history in compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies.

What data is deleted:

  • Your user profile, username, bio, and avatar.
  • All published pulses, comments, and clips.
  • Saved spatial radar coordinates and proximity history.
  • Local session credentials, tokens, and direct message caches.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Localiiiy app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the profile or settings icon and select Privacy Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap "Delete Account & Wipe Data".
  4. Confirm your request. All local Room records and active session data will be permanently wiped immediately.
