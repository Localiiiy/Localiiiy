🛡️ Official Legal Disclosure & Store Compliance

Privacy Policy & Community Guidelines

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 2.4.0 • Google Play & App Store Production Compliant

Section 1.0 — Overview & Core Privacy Principles

Localiiiy ("we", "us", "our", or "the Platform") is a next-generation hyperlocal social discovery, creator clips, and neighborhood marketplace platform accessible via our Android Application and official Firebase Web App (https://localiiiy.web.app).

We believe social networking should be authentic, privacy-first, and respectful. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers or third-party behavioral advertising networks.

Section 2.0 — Google Play Store Data Safety Disclosures

Google Play Developer Program Compliance: The following declarations reflect the exact data collected, processed, and secured in Localiiiy for all real users.

A. Personal Information

  • Display Name, Username & Email: Processed securely via Firebase Authentication (Google Cloud) strictly for user authentication, security verification, and account recovery.
  • Account Identifiers: Randomly generated cryptographic UUIDs used to manage your local profile, posts, and community badges.

B. Location Telemetry & Dynamic Proximity Radar

  • Approximate & Precise Location: Used exclusively for real-time proximity discovery when the user actively switches ON the Proximity Radar or uses "Nearby" filters.
  • Zero Background Tracking: Location coordinates are never polled, tracked, or broadcast when the application is closed or in the background.
  • Off-Grid / Ghost Cloak (Stealth Observer Mode): Users can browse without broadcasting their GPS pin or appearing on nearby radars.
  • Spatial Obfuscation (Radar Masks): Users can blur their coordinates to a 3km, 5km, or 10km radius grid. Minors (under 18) are protected with mandatory location obfuscation.

C. Photos, Videos & Media

  • Zero-Permission Photo Picker: Localiiiy utilizes Android's native system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The application never requests broad storage access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).
  • Creator Content: Media uploaded for posts, clips, or marketplace listings is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections and stored securely in Google Cloud Firebase Storage.

D. What We NEVER Collect

  • We NEVER access or scrape your contact address book.
  • We NEVER listen to your microphone or wiretap calls.
  • We NEVER inspect SMS messages or personal files on your device.
  • We NEVER collect or store credit card numbers, bank PINs, or raw financial instruments.

Section 3.0 — Real User Account Deletion & Right to Permanent Erasure

In strict compliance with Google Play Store User Data & Account Deletion policies:

  1. 1-Tap In-App Erasure: Any real user can permanently wipe their account at any time by navigating to: Profile → Settings → Delete Account (Complete Data Oblivion).
  2. Instant Irrevocable Purge: Deleting your account immediately erases:
    • Your Firebase Authentication account credentials.
    • Your Firestore user profile, post records, clips, and messages.
    • All local SQLite Room database tables and cached media on your device.
  3. Web / Email Deletion Request: If you cannot access the app, you can submit an automated deletion request via our privacy portal at https://localiiiy.web.app/privacy or by emailing support@localiiiy.web.app.

Section 4.0 — Dual-Reach Creator Distribution & Content Sovereignty

Localiiiy provides creators complete control over their content distribution:

  • Hyperlocal Anchoring (Neighbor): Content first surfaces in your immediate neighborhood, enabling authentic discoverability without requiring large follower counts.
  • Algorithmic Scaling (Neighbor to Earth): Engaging clips naturally graduate through: Neighbor → Neighborhood → City → State → Earth (Worldwide).
  • Creator Sovereignty: Creators can choose to constrain content locally or enable global worldwide reach before publishing.

Section 5.0 — Community Safety, Anti-Harassment & Zero-Tolerance UGC Policy

Apple Guideline 1.2 & Google Play UGC Compliance: Localiiiy maintains zero tolerance for abusive, objectionable, or harmful content.

  • Prohibited Content: Violence, hate speech, sexual exploitation, harassment, illicit substances, weapons, and scam listings are strictly forbidden.
  • Instant 1-Tap Flagging & Reporting: Every post, clip, and user profile includes a visible report button dispatched immediately to our 24-hour moderation queue.
  • Instant 2-Way Blocking: Blocking any user immediately removes them from your live radar, feed, and messaging channels.
  • 24-Hour Moderation SLA: Violations result in immediate content takedown and permanent account termination.

Section 6.0 — Creator Monetization, Bounties & Worldwide Payouts

Localiiiy supports creator monetization through verified ad revenue sharing (55% creator share), direct fan tips (0% platform commission), and marketplace services.

  • Minimum Withdrawal Threshold: To prevent financial fraud, the baseline minimum payout threshold is 1,000.00 USD or the exact live exchange equivalent in your country's local currency.
  • Disbursement Channels: Payouts are routed through verified international networks (Stripe Connect, PayPal, SWIFT Bank Wire, SEPA, UPI) upon completing standard identity verification.
