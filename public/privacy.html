🛰️ Localiiiy Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Localiiiy is built on the foundation of Hyperlocal Trust and User Privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring complete transparency regarding how spatial coordinates and user interactions are handled.

1. Spatial Location & Proximity Data

Localiiiy uses your device's coarse or precise location only to discover nearby community posts, spatial radar blips, and marketplace listings within your selected radius (1 km to Earth scale).

Ghost / Stealth Mode: You can enable Free Master Ghost Mode at any time to browse and observe radar activity without broadcasting your coordinates or presence to other users.

2. Safe-Haven Escrow & Commerce

Marketplace interactions occur directly between local community members with optional Safe-Haven verification. Localiiiy never sells your financial or purchase records to third-party ad networks.

3. Account Deletion & Right to be Forgotten

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and wipe all personal logs directly within the app under Settings > Privacy Settings > Delete Account & Wipe Data, or via our web deletion portal at Account Deletion Page.
